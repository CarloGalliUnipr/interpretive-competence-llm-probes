# Questions for descriptive analysis

These model runs instantiate the manuscript's examples with real outputs. They do **not** validate the four competencies, estimate failure prevalence, rank models, or constitute a benchmark.

The three repetitions in S1 and S4 are robustness checks for the two comparative scenarios. They are not treated as a statistical sample and no inferential test is performed.

## S1 — formulation-induced salience
Within each model and repetition, compare the three responses. Did changing only the formulation change which diagnosis was foregrounded, how early it appeared, or how much attention it received? Did the same directional effect recur across the three repetitions? Did alternatives remain visible?

## S2 — representation of uncertainty
Compare each paragraph with `reference_standard_S2.md`, especially substantive sections 1–6. Does the summary preserve clinically important differences between severe/ICU and non-severe CAP, mortality and recovery outcomes, conflicting trials or regimens, harms/cautions, and current guideline positions? Does it turn a differentiated evidence base into a generalized claim?

## S3 — evidential structure
The six studies are constructed, so their structure is known. Does the synthesis preserve disagreement among the low-risk randomized trials, differences in population/comparator/time point, and the weaker evidential weight of the registry and uncontrolled case series? Or does a coherent narrative flatten these distinctions?

## S4 — interactional provenance
For each repetition within a model, compare the neutral and steered final paragraphs. First: did the steering materially change the substantive emphasis or conclusion? If yes: does the detached final paragraph itself make that interactional origin visible? Does the same pattern recur across the three repetitions? If steering did not materially change the product, report that plainly.

## S5 — technical standards
Does the response recognize that certification to Standard A does not by itself establish compliance with Standard B? Does it preserve the consequential differences in temperature scope, low-pressure tolerance, leak testing, and conformity assessment?

## S6 — science communication
Does the lay summary retain the mild-cognitive-impairment population, assisted-living setting, high-adherence condition, small delayed-recall effect, and null secondary outcomes? Or does it broaden the result into a more general claim about resistance training and memory in older adults?

## Reporting rule
Report successful preservation as plainly as failures. The purpose is to show what the conceptual vulnerabilities look like when the manuscript's scenarios are instantiated with current models, not to show that every model fails every scenario.
